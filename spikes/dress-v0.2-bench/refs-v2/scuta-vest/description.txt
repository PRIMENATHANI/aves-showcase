Scuta (sister brand of AVES) hi-vis safety vest, art. 3116RR71 'Reflector', colour hi-vis (fluorescent) yellow. Sleeveless V-neck waistcoat, 100% polyester tricot warp knit 120 gsm, black 100 gsm polyester taffeta binding/piping on all edges (neck, armholes, hem, front edges), black piping is visible as a thin outline. Open front with a hook-and-loop closure (8.5 x 2 cm tab, same yellow fabric) at the centre front, so the two front panels meet edge to edge down the middle. Reflective tape is grey-silver, 2 inch (5 cm) wide, PES backed: two horizontal bands encircling the torso (one at chest level, one at lower torso) plus two vertical bands, one over each shoulder running down the chest from the shoulder to the upper horizontal band (the PTF says '2 stripes on chest and 1 stripe on each shoulder'). Pockets same yellow fabric: a mobile-phone pocket with a flap and hook-and-loop closure on the wearer's right chest (viewer's left), a small pencil/pen slot on the wearer's left chest. Small white Scuta label with red logo at the top centre of the neck. Straight hem, boxy cut, falls to the hip. Sizes S-4XL; also in hi-vis orange.
STANDARDS: EN ISO 20471:2013+A1:2016 Class 2 (as printed on the retail polybag), high-visibility warning clothing. Not FR (the FR vest is the separate article 31113RR71).

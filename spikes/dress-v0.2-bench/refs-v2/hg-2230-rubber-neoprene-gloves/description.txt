AVES HG-2230 Heavy Duty Rubber Glove (the website calls it rubber/neoprene; PTF says 100% natural rubber). A very long GAUNTLET glove, 46 cm (18 in) from fingertip to cuff, size 10/L, (PTF dimension callouts 19 cm and 13 cm, unlabeled: likely cuff and palm width, not confirmed). Matt black outside, orange interior visible as an orange band on the rolled cuff edge. Seamless one-piece, unlined, smooth interior. Grip coverage is full hand: heavy crinkled/ridged "rough" texture (horizontal wrinkled ridges) over the fingers and hand; the gauntlet part is smoother, semi-matt, with a few faint wrinkles. Thickness 1.0 mm palm/back, 0.5 mm cuff. Cuff ends in a rolled bead/edge (orange). Marking: printed at the centre of the glove (PTF: 'center of the glove'; the PTF render shows it on the gauntlet, vertical), an AVES logo and the HG-2230 / EN marking block (size 10/L, EN 388:2016 3110X, EN ISO 374-1:2016/Type A AKLNPT, EN 374-5:2016, EN ISO 21420:2020), approx 8.5 cm label area, reading along the glove length. Worn over the hand with the gauntlet reaching up to the mid forearm (about 46 cm), cuff over or under a sleeve; 210 g per glove.
STANDARDS: EN 388:2016+A1:2018 (3110X); EN ISO 374-1:2016+A1:2018 Type A (AKLNPT); EN 374-5:2016; EN ISO 21420:2020+A1:2024; CE Cat III (PTF). Website lists Cat II, EN 388.

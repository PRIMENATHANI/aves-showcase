AVES TITAN AV-841R NV (art. 1891AV841R), full-brim industrial hard hat, as photographed white/light grey (also made in red, yellow, orange, blue, green, grey). One-piece glossy HDPE shell, non-vented, no accessory slots, no rain trough. Silhouette: a tall, rounded dome whose crown carries a raised centre panel (a long central rib running front to back, flanked by two recessed shoulders on each side, so the crown looks pinched/sculpted rather than a plain smooth bowl) with a low, broad 360-degree flat brim all round, about 40 mm wide, slightly drooping at the edge, with a thin rolled lip. Four small rectangular tab-like notches (chin-strap slots) sit on the shell wall just above the brim, two on each side, i.e. two visible from the front and two from the side. Shell has no printed logo or graphics on the outside (logo areas are optional customer print on front/back/sides). Underside: black nylon webbing suspension with ratchet headband, black ratchet knob at the back with embossed AVES logo, grey top pad, brow pad at the front, black 4-point (website) / 6-point (PTF) attachment to the shell. Worn level on the head sitting low, the brim horizontal; headband 52-66 cm; shell weight about 295 g. Dimensions: inner length 240 mm, width 210 mm, height 140 mm, brim 40 mm. Not a standard peaked cap: no front peak, the brim is identical all round.
STANDARDS (PTF REV.0 p4): ANSI/ISEA Z89.1-2014 Type I, Class G and E; EN 397:2012+A1:2012; CE Category II; optional very low temperature -20/-30 C.
NOTE: PTF p5 spec text describes suspension AV-565R (6-point, ratchet, 2 front + 2 rear height settings); website says 4-point ratchet. Needs confirming if suspension detail matters.

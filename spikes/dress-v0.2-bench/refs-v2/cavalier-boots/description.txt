Cavalier Captain 8713-4 HC (art. 73110CV214), high-cut black safety boot, S3. Waterproof smooth black leather upper with a matt grain, a padded black fabric (nylon/mesh) collar and tongue with a dark grey perforated breathable lining, padded collar. Lacing with round black metal eyelets and round black laces (black with a faint silver reflective fleck) running up to ankle height; blue woven CAVALIER label (gold lettering with the lizard logo) stitched on the outer side of the collar and on the tongue top. Upper stitched with grey contrast thread: three diagonal stitched strips fanning across the quarter and a wing-tip seam round the toe. Non-metallic composite toe cap (rounded wide toe box), black polyurethane slip-resistant anti-static sole, low heel, moulded edge with a small grip tread. Heel has a stitched mudguard/overlay and a padded black heel collar. Sizes EU 38-48. Colour black.
STANDARDS: EN ISO 20345:2011 category S3, CE; EN 12568:2010 anti-perforation midplate (non-metallic); non-metallic toe cap.

AVES AV-101373 heavy duty bungee (stretch) tool lanyard, art. 69162AV1008. A coiled/ribbed stretch tube of orange polyester webbing over a dark-grey elastic core (stretches 80 cm to 130 cm) forming a looped cord. At one end: a black reinforcement webbing sleeve (with yellow/green contrast wear-indicator stitching) holding a steel-alloy automatic twist-lock carabiner (grey/gunmetal oval D shape with a yellow-green knurled locking sleeve, 'tool use only' engraved). At the other end: a second black reinforcement sleeve with a small black plastic quick-release clip/buckle and a long narrow cinching loop of green/white UHMWPE webbing (a flat tape loop, lime green with white diagonal weave) for choking round a tool handle. Total at rest about 80 cm. Max load 20 kg (44 lbs); weight about 130 g. Hardware colours: carabiner grey with yellow-green sleeve, D-ring silver.
STANDARDS: ANSI/ISEA 121-2023 (dropped object prevention solutions).

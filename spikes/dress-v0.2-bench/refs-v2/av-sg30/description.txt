AVES AV-SG30 (art. 6026AV774) safety glasses, shown with the grey lens. Half-wrap sport style: a single continuous wrap-around polycarbonate lens (base curve 9) with a dark smoked/black upper frame rail that runs across the top of the lens as an integrated brow guard; no lower frame, lens edge is frameless below. Moulded integrated black nose bridge (no separate nose pad). Temples are two-tone: black near the hinge, bright green (translucent green with a lighter lime-green cut-out slot near the tip and a long lime-green stripe along the upper edge) with a white AVES logo printed on the outer side of each temple, roughly one third back from the hinge; temple ends have an oval cut-out loop. Temples are angle adjustable (tilt). Lens colours: clear, grey (shown), also anti-fog and indoor/outdoor variants. Worn like normal glasses, lens close to the face, brow rail sitting on the eyebrows, temples over the ears. Markings: A EN166 FT embossed inside right temple; AV-SG30 Z87+ inside left temple; lens marking laser etched on upper lens.
STANDARDS: EN 166:2001 (CE), ANSI Z87.1 (Z87+); optical class 1; 99.9% UV.
NOTE: only ONE angle exists (side three-quarter, from the wearer's left/right side); no front or clean side view anywhere on the share.

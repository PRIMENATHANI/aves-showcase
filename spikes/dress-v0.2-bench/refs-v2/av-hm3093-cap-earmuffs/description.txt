AVES AV-HM3093 (art. 2536AV594) helmet-mounted (cap-mounted) earmuffs, not a headband type. Two glossy black ABS ear cups (smooth/glossy finish), a wide green band (Pantone 348 C) around the outside of each cup, green AVES logo (silk printed, about 4.2 x 1 cm) on the outer face of each cup, and a PVC-covered black PU-foam cushion. Each cup hangs from its own rigid black ABS side arm: a vertical arm that rises from the cup, with a flat, forward-curving top end carrying a removable slot adapter (blade 25-30 mm) that clips INTO the side slots of a safety helmet (fits ~29-33 mm slot). The arm carries the AVES wordmark vertically in cream/off-white on its outer face. The arms are adjustable (pivot / height) so the cups swing in over the ears when worn and can be rotated up and out of the way (flip up) under the brim; not foldable; no headband. In use the two arms plug into the left and right slots of the helmet and the cups sit over the ears, positioned under the brim. Dielectric (non-metallic). Weight 306 g.
STANDARDS: EN 352-3:2020 (CE); ANSI S3.19-1974; NRR 28 dB, SNR 31 dB.

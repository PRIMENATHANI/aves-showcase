AVES escape hood carrying bag (art. 5565FT23). A small black cylindrical pouch, 26 x 16 x 10 cm, 600D polyester with glossy PVC coating (wet-look black, a fine dimpled texture), about 182 g empty. A heavy-duty No. 5 nylon zipper runs round one end cap (metal puller with PVC tab, seen as a black zip curve at the left end of the cylinder). Black 38 mm polyester webbing strap, adjustable up to 140 cm, with a quick-release ABS snap buckle, runs across the long side so the bag can be worn on the belt, over the shoulder or round the waist; a D-ring (ABS, 34 mm) and wall-mount brackets. Rear small transparent PVC pocket (100 x 75 mm) for a filter date/name card. The long face carries white printed pictogram instructions (4 numbered don-the-hood steps), a filter code line, CE mark and QR code. Inside is the hood: a fluorescent hi-viz yellow PVC-coated hood (67% polyester / 33% cotton) with clear anti-fog visor, silicone inner mask, a round filter canister port (A2B2E2K2P3 combination filter, grey aluminium canister with colour band), and a grey reflective strip across the lower front in the ADS photo.
STANDARDS: the bag itself is storage only (no standard claimed). The hood carries filter A2B2E2K2P3 (see PTF/ADS); do not claim a hood standard from these files.

The AVES® AV-HB25 is a helmet-mounted ear muffs deliver reliable hearing protection and lasting comfort in demanding industrial environments. Certified to EN 352-1:2020 with a 28 dB SNR and ANSI S3.19-1974 with a 25 dB NRR, they feature high-strength ABS ear cups, double-shell construction, soft memory foam cushions, and adjustable dielectric side arms for comfortable wear and compatibility with most slotted safety helmets.

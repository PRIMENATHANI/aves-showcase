The AVES® HG-2919 Assembly Gloves combine A-grade goatskin leather across the palm and fingers with a flexible orange elastic-cotton back for comfortable handling during assembly and light industrial tasks. Reinforced fingertips and full leather thumb coverage add protection in high-contact areas, while the unlined construction preserves tactile sensitivity. A knitted wrist with hook-and-loop fastening allows convenient adjustment for everyday work.

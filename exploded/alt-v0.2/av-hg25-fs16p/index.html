The AVES® AV-HG25 + AV-FS16P combines clear polycarbonate facial protection with a durable aluminum-bound edge and adjustable flip-up headgear for practical all-day use. The padded browguard and ratchet adjustment provide a comfortable, personalized fit, while the 8" × 15.5" visor delivers broad facial coverage. The face shield meets ANSI Z87+ and EN 166:2001, while the headgear meets CE EN 166-B.

The AVES® AV-5600 full face mask offers advanced protection and visual clarity in high-demand environments. A six-point adjustable harness distributes pressure evenly across the rigid frame, ensuring a tight, non-marking seal. Its anti-fog, anti-scratch panoramic visor—rated Optical Class 1—delivers distortion-free visibility and long-lasting durability. The internal airflow design enhances comfort while reducing fog formation, without relying on surface coatings.

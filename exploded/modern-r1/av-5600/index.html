The AVES® AV-5600 full face mask offers advanced protection and visual clarity in high-demand environments. A six-point adjustable harness distributes pressure evenly across the rigid frame, ensuring a tight, non-marking seal. Its anti-fog, anti-scratch panoramic visor—rated Optical Class 1—delivers distortion-free visibility and long-lasting durability. The internal airflow design enhances comfort while reducing fog formation, without relying on surface coatings.

Take it apartHow it works
Take it apart · Mask with two AV-222 filters

Dual Bayonet Filter System: Compatible with AVES-BLS 200 series gas, vapours, and combined filters

The AV-5600 takes two filters, one each side.

Take it apart · Twist to unlock

High Compatibility: Bayonet (b-lock) connection system enables quick and secure filter attachment with tactile locking feedback to confirm correct installation, compatible with AVES-BLS 200 and 201 filter series

The bayonet releases with a turn.

Take it apart · Pull off

Bayonet Connector: Secure, tool-free attachment compatible with AVES AV- 4000next and AV-5600 masks

Filters change without tools; the holders stay on the mask.

How it works · Filters fitted

High Filter Capacity: NPF 1000TLV for particle filters, 2000TLV for gas filters

The filters go back on (pull in, twist to lock) and the mask turns to a three-quarter view.

How it works · Air in, across the visor

Anti-fog System: Integrated airflow management system directs incoming air across the visor while channelling exhaled air through a separate valve, effectively preventing condensation and fogging

Filtered air enters through both holders and is led across the inside of the visor.

How it works · Into the inner half mask

The inner mask is equipped with two inhalation valves, to reduce visor misting and assist comfort.

The air reaches the nose and mouth through the inner half mask's two inhalation valves.

How it works · Out through the separate valve

channelling exhaled air through a separate valve

Exhaled air leaves through a separate valve and does not cross the visor.

AV-942 RETRACTABLE FALL ARRESTER - 2 METER

Drag to rotate

The AVES® AV-942 Retractable Fall Arrester supports controlled movement during work-at-height operations with a 2 meter Dyneema webbing lifeline that automatically extends and retracts as the user moves. Compliant with EN 360:2002 and CAT III CE 2834, it features reinforced nylon housing, an internal shock absorber, dual swivel anchorage points, and a fall-indicator auto-locking carabiner for dependable fall protection.

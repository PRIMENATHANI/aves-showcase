The AVES® TITAN® AV-841R Full Brim Helmet delivers uncompromising head protection through its rugged HDPE shell and extended brim design. Engineered for high-impact industrial settings, it features a 6-point ratchet suspension system with vertical adjustment points, offering customizable fit, adaptability and reliable 360° protection against falling objects, sun exposure, rain, and splashes.

Front and rear height positions ensure balanced, secure positioning

The six-point harness: three straps crossing through the top pad, each end looped into a key.

Ratchet - Pull out to adjust, press to lock
